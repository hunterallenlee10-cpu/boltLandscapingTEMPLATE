How a cleanup day goes

One visit, four steps, no mess left.

  1. Walk the property

    Your crew lead checks beds, drains, gutters and anything fragile, and confirms the plan with you by text before work starts.

  2. Clear every corner

    Blowers for the open lawn, rakes and hands for the beds. We get under decks, along fences and out of window wells.

  3. Haul it to compost

    Leaves leave in our truck, not in your trash. Everything goes to a local composting yard and comes back as mulch.

  4. Leave it winter-ready

    A final mow, edges cleaned, hard surfaces blown off. You get before and after photos the same afternoon.

When should I book my fall cleanup?

Most trees here drop between late October and the third week of November. Book by mid-October to lock in a date. Season plan visits are scheduled for you.

Do you take the leaves away?

Yes. Every leaf leaves in our truck and goes to a local composting yard. Haul-away is included in every quote.

What if more leaves fall after my visit?

The season plan includes three visits timed to the drop. One-time customers can add a follow-up visit at 20% off within 30 days.

Can you mulch the leaves into the lawn instead?

For light cover, yes. Shredded leaves feed the soil. Heavy cover smothers grass, so we remove it and mulch only what the lawn can take.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave the gate unlocked and we will text you when we arrive, when we finish, and send photos of the result.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We carry full liability and workers' comp coverage, and we are happy to send certificates to you or your HOA.
